## \# 📱 Social Media Engagement Intelligence 2026

## 

## \## 📊 Project Overview

## 

## Social Media Engagement Intelligence 2026 is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze social media performance and audience engagement.

## 

## The project analyzes 100 social media posts to identify high-performing platforms, content types, topics, and individual posts.

## 

## \## 🎯 Objectives

## 

## \- Analyze engagement performance across platforms

## \- Identify high-performing content types

## \- Compare views, likes, comments, shares, and saves

## \- Analyze engagement trends

## \- Identify high-performing topics

## 

## \## 🛠️ Tools \& Technologies

## 

## \- Microsoft Power BI

## \- MySQL

## \- Microsoft Excel

## \- Power Query

## \- DAX

## \- SQL

## \- Data Visualization

## 

## \## 📈 Dashboard Features

## 

## \- Total Views KPI

## \- Total Likes KPI

## \- Total Comments KPI

## \- Total Shares KPI

## \- Average Engagement Rate KPI

## \- Engagement Rate by Platform

## \- Views vs Engagement Rate by Topic

## \- Views by Content Type

## \- Platform \& Content Type Engagement Matrix

## \- Platform Slicer

## \- Topic Slicer

## \- Content Type Slicer

## \- Post Date Slicer

## 

## \## 🔍 Key Insights

## 

## \- Platform performance varies significantly based on engagement rate.

## \- Different content types generate different levels of audience reach.

## \- Some topics achieve high views but do not necessarily have the highest engagement.

## \- Platform and content-type combinations help identify stronger content strategies.

## 

## \## 💡 Business Value

## 

## This dashboard can help social media teams:

## 

## \- Identify high-performing content

## \- Understand audience engagement behavior

## \- Compare platform performance

## \- Improve content strategy

## \- Focus on topics and content types that generate stronger engagement
