### **# AI Job Market Intelligence 2026**



###### \## 📊 Project Overview



AI Job Market Intelligence 2026 is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze job-market trends using a dataset of 20 job postings.



The dashboard provides insights into job demand, required skills, locations, experience levels, and work modes.



##### \## 🎯 Objectives



\- Analyze job demand by job title

\- Identify the most demanded skills

\- Analyze job locations

\- Understand experience-level requirements

\- Analyze remote, hybrid, and onsite work modes

\- Present insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard



###### \## 🛠️ Tools \& Technologies



\- Microsoft Power BI

\- Power Query

\- Microsoft Excel

\- SQL

\- Data Visualization



###### \## 📈 Dashboard Insights



\- Power BI and SQL are among the most demanded skills.

\- India and Pune are major job locations in the dataset.

\- Most jobs have unspecified experience requirements.

\- Remote and flexible work options are present.

\- The dashboard contains 20 job postings.



###### \## 📊 Dashboard Features



\- Total Jobs KPI

\- AI Jobs

\- SQL Jobs

\- Python Jobs

\- Power BI Jobs

\- Excel Jobs

\- Job Demand by Job Title

\- Job Demand by Experience Level

\- Job Demand by Location

\- Job Demand by Work Mode

\- Top Skills in Job Market

\- Key Insights section
